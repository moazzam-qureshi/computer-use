AI Voice Agents

I build inbound voice agents using ElevenLabs Conversational AI, connected through custom MCP tool servers for sub-second data access during live calls. These systems are tenant-scoped, role-aware, and integrated directly into production backend systems.

RAG and Document Intelligence

I build production RAG and agentic RAG systems for enterprise search, knowledge bases, and document-heavy workflows. This includes VLM-based page parsing for complex layouts, hybrid BM25 and vector search, and continuous sync with OneDrive, Google Drive, and SharePoint where needed.

LLM Applications and AI SaaS Features

I integrate LLMs directly into SaaS products - including AI copilots, AI-powered workflows, and automated communication over email, SMS, and WhatsApp. This includes OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, OpenRouter, and Ollama depending on the use case.
